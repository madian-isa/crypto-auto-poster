Crypto Signal Auto-Poster (Binance Square)Automatically reads setup/signal data (from your backtest/signal generator), writes an honest, fact-based technical breakdown draft in the agreed house style, sends it to your Telegram for a quick Approve/Reject tap, and only then publishes it to Binance Square.FlowReads Trade Setups: Automatically fetches current trade setups (e.g., from backtest_setups.json or your signal engine).Writes a Draft: Formats entry, targets, stop-loss, and technical analysis (rule-based, free — no Anthropic key strictly needed).Telegram Approval: Sends the draft to your Telegram with ✅ Approve / ❌ Reject inline buttons.Waits for Response: Waits up to APPROVAL_TIMEOUT_MINUTES (default 60) for your tap.Publishes: If approved → posts the technical signal to Binance Square and sends the published link back to Telegram.Cleans Up/Skips: If rejected or no response in time → skips it and updates status without posting.What it does NOT do (by design)No clickbait or emotional hooks ("100x guaranteed", "is your money safe", "final pump", etc.).No guaranteed profit promises or exaggerated claims.No third-party charts/videos attached without validation — generates standard data breakdowns.Stays conservative on posting frequency (default max 6/day, randomized timing) to avoid spam flags on Binance Square.Filessrc/
  config.py             - reads all secrets from environment (never hard-coded)
  signal_source.py      - reads trading setups/signals (e.g. backtest_setups.json or API)
  content_generator.py  - formats headline, entry/targets, and technical breakdown
  post_template.py      - assembles final text: 2000 char limit, cashtags (e.g. $BTC), #hashtags
  square_post.py        - publishes to Binance Square via the official OpenAPI endpoint
  telegram_approval.py  - sends draft + Approve/Reject buttons, handles inline action clicks
  dedupe.py             - tracks what setup IDs have already been processed/posted
  main.py               - the loop: check signals -> draft -> Telegram approval -> post -> repeat
